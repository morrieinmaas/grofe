# Framed panes, gaps and moving panes with the mouse

**Date:** 2026-11-05
**Status:** implemented (milestone MF in `docs/plans/2026-09-23-milestones.md`,
FR.1 to FR.5); owner-approved design: framed and rounded by default, gap only,
no padding.
**Inspiration:** tiling window managers with gaps (niri, i3-gaps) and tuios's
pane drag and drop.

## Goal

Under grofe's own identity a window is a canvas: tiled panes are rounded,
framed tiles with a small gap between them, and any number of a window's panes
can float above the tiles. Panes move with the mouse: drag a tile onto another
to swap or dock it, float a pane and drop it back into the tiling.

Under tmux identity (spec D12) nothing changes: shared one-cell borders, tmux's
geometry, and every compat golden byte for byte. The framed look is only the
grofe-identity default; both looks stay options either way.

## Options

All window scope, grofe names. Through tmux's CLI (`set-option`, `show-options`)
they are unknown under the `tmux` name, as any grofe-only option is. **In
grofe's TOML config they are accepted under both identities** (owner review
2026-11-05): the owner runs grofe as `tmux`, and `pane-frames = true` plus
`pane-gap` in `config.toml` must turn the framed canvas on there too. Only the
defaults differ by identity (table below).

| Option | Values | grofe default | tmux identity default |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pane-frames` | `on`, `off` | `on` | `off` |
| `pane-gap` | cells, 0 to 8 | `1` | `0` |
| `pane-frame-title` | `on`, `off` | `on` | `on` |
| `pane-frame-tab-ends` | two one-cell glyphs | `▐▌` | `▐▌` (no tab is drawn) |

- `pane-border-lines` gains `rounded` (tmux's popup set already has it). With
  `pane-frames on` the default is `rounded`; with frames off it stays `single`.
  `rounded` uses `╭ ╮ ╰ ╯ ─ │` for frames. With frames off, `rounded` draws as
  `single` (shared borders have junctions a rounded corner cannot draw).
- Styles are tmux's: `pane-border-style` for a frame, `pane-active-border-style`
  for the active pane's frame and for the drop preview.
- The frame title is `pane-border-format`, drawn inside the top edge of the
  frame, `╭─ 1: nvim ─────╮`. It costs no row: there is no separate title bar.
  `pane-frame-title off` draws a plain top edge. With frames on,
  `pane-border-status` has no effect (the title already has a place).

## Geometry

With `pane-frames on` and gap `g`:

- Each tiled pane's content rect is surrounded by its own one-cell frame.
- Between two neighbouring frames there are `g` empty cells, and there are `g`
  empty cells between the outer frames and the window's edge (niri's
  "outer gap = inner gap"; one option, not two).
- So the separator between two content rects is `2 + g` cells (frame, gap,
  frame), where tmux's is 1, and the content area is the window inset by
  `1 + g` on each side.
- The layout tree keeps its structure and every operation (`split-window`,
  `resize-pane -L 5`, presets, `select-layout`, swap, rotate, join) works as
  before, in cells of content. `#{pane_width}` and the pty size are the content
  size. `#{window_layout}` describes the content rects.
- A zoomed pane fills the window as one framed tile with the same gap.
- A window too small to frame every pane (a content rect would drop below 1x1)
  falls back to shared borders for that redraw. Mark it `// ponytail:`.
- Floating panes keep their own geometry rules (`docs/floating-pane-mouse.md`)
  and draw their frame in the same style, `rounded` by default.

## Drawing

- Each tile's frame is drawn around its content rect, active pane in
  `pane-active-border-style`, the others in `pane-border-style`.
- Gap cells are drawn as the window's default background (no glyph).
- Floating panes are drawn above the tiles, frame included, as now.
- The drop preview (below) is a frame drawn in `pane-active-border-style` around
  the rect the dragged pane will land in, drawn above tiles and below floating
  panes.
- A pane being moved (below) is drawn as a floating frame under the pointer,
  above everything, and its own place shows empty until the release.

### The control tab (grofe identity, owner review 2026-11-09)

With `mouse` on and `pane-frame-title on`, the top edge of every frame (tiles
and floating panes) ends in a control tab, one edge cell before the right
corner: `╭─ 1 "nvim" ───── ▐z f x▌ ─╮`. The title is cut before it.

- Its ends are `pane-frame-tab-ends`' two glyphs, drawn in the frame's style;
  the controls between are drawn in it reversed. The tab is a pill of the
  frame's own colour (the active one's `pane-active-border-style`), on a light
  theme or a dark one. The default `▐▌` is in every terminal font; a Powerline
  or Nerd font's rounded `U+E0B6 U+E0B4` are the usual choice there. Anything
  but two one-cell glyphs gives plain ends.
- `z` (`u` on a zoomed window) is `MouseDown1Control8`, tmux's stock
  `resize-pane -Z`; `f` (`t` on a floating pane) is `MouseDown1Control7`,
  bound under grofe identity to float the pane or put it back in the tiling;
  `x` is `MouseDown1Control9`, tmux's stock row, which asks before killing.
- The default `pane-border-format`'s `[z][x]` buttons give way to the tab: a
  framed title with the format left at its default draws it less those buttons.
  A format of your own is drawn as it is, with the tab after it.
- A frame too narrow for the tab and the title's start draws no tab.
- Under tmux identity no tab is drawn and `MouseDown1Control7` is unbound:
  frames on, the title is tmux's stock `pane-border-format`, `[z][x]` and all.

## The mouse (needs `mouse on`)

Keys stay tmux's, so bindings keep working: a press or drag on any frame cell or
gap cell is `MouseDown1Border` / `MouseDrag1Border`, aimed at the pane the cell
belongs to (gap cells belong to the nearest frame; a tie goes to the pane on the
left or top, as tmux's border ownership does).

The built-in behaviour of tmux's stock row (`resize-pane -M` on
`MouseDrag1Border`) on a framed tile:

| Where the drag starts | What it does |
|---|---|
| a tile's top edge (its title) | moves the pane (drag and drop, below) |
| any other frame edge or a gap cell between tiles | resizes, as a tmux border drag does today |
| a floating pane's border | as today (`docs/floating-pane-mouse.md`) |

`C-MouseDrag1Pane` (Ctrl and drag anywhere in a pane) is bound by default under
grofe identity to `drag-pane -M`, a new grofe verb that starts the same move as
dragging the title. Under tmux identity the key is unbound, as in tmux.

### Drag and drop

While the button is down the pane is lifted (owner review 2026-11-09): it is
drawn as a floating frame, content live, at the pointer, the cell grabbed kept
under the pointer, above everything; its place in the tiling shows empty. Each
motion report moves it and redraws; the layout and the pty size do not change
until the release. The drop preview shows where the pane will land. On release
over a tiled pane `T` (not the dragged pane itself):

| Release over | Result |
|---|---|
| the middle of `T` | the two panes swap places (as `swap-pane`) |
| the outer quarter of one of `T`'s sides | `T` is split, and the dragged pane takes the half on that side; the space it left is filled as `kill-pane` would fill it |
| the dragged pane itself | nothing changes |
| no tile (a gap, the window's edge) | the pane floats where it was dropped, at its size, on top, and is the active pane; a floating pane moves there. A window's last tiled pane stays put. Without frames only a drop off the window floats |

- A right click, or Escape, before the release cancels the move: the pane is
  drawn in its place again and the rest of the drag does nothing.

- An edge drop that would leave the layout as it is changes nothing, so a moved
  divider stays put.
- A pane too small to split along an axis offers no edges on that axis.
- A floating pane dragged by its title moves, as today. Ctrl-drag of a floating
  pane (`drag-pane -M`) drops it into the tiling by the table above: that is
  unfloating with the mouse.
- A zoomed window does not start a move.
- The program inside learns its new size once, on release.

## Floating and unfloating

- `float-pane` exists (tiled to floating).
- New: `unfloat-pane [-t pane] [-s target-pane] [-h|-v]`: a floating pane goes
  back into the tiling, splitting the tiled pane under its centre (or
  `-s target-pane`, or the window's last active tiled pane), along the longer
  axis of that pane unless `-h` or `-v` says. Process and history are kept.
- New: `drag-pane -M`, the mouse move above (only meaningful from a mouse key).
- Default binding under grofe identity: `prefix F` toggles the active pane,
  `if -F '#{pane_floating_flag}' 'unfloat-pane' 'float-pane'`. The default pane
  menu (`MouseDown3Pane`) under grofe identity gains Float and Unfloat items.
  Under tmux identity the stock table and menu are unchanged.

## Not in this milestone

Animations (the lifted pane jumps from report to report), a drop-preview delay, snap-to-half keys for floating panes,
minimising, and padding inside frames (owner ruling: gap only).
